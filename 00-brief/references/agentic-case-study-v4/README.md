# Control Hub Agentic, v4: reference-led redesign

November 12, 2026. The case study is redesigned in the language of the Monograph homepage and the open-notebook works page. Each section was designed against three references from the inspiration library (`~/google-interview/presentation-style/inspiration/`, `manifest.json`), with one move taken from each. A move is a principle, never a copied graphic, palette or typeface.

- `plan.json`: each section's job and its three references.
- `study-*.jpg`: one study sheet per section (the three images with their Borrow / Adapt / Avoid notes).
- `before-after-*.jpg`: the same viewport of the v3 page and the v4 page.

The v3 page is kept, untouched, at `/works/control-hub-agentic?v=3` for comparison. `/works/control-hub-agentic` serves v4.

## The construction rules

- **Screens are tipped onto cloth.** Every Control Hub screen is dark, and a dark screen on paper read as a hole in the page. Each one is now mounted on a board of the notebook's navy cloth, the same cloth as the homepage cover, the way a photograph is tipped onto a book cover: the screen whole and centred, equal margins at the sides and top, a slightly deeper margin below. Nothing is printed over the screen. Screens are never cropped and never shown wider than their capture.
- **Two margins, two jobs.** The left margin (columns 1–3) says where you are: chapter numerals, prototype numerals, labels. The right margin (columns 10–12) holds supporting evidence: the one figure beside the prose. The text column is columns 4–9.
- **One chapter mark.** Every chapter opens the same way the works page's *Contents* does: a mono numeral, one italic word, a full ink rule, then one claim.
- **One rule break.** "Should we pause?" is the only line on the page set at poster scale.
- **Four type sizes.** Every serif line is one of four steps: XL (the title, "Should we pause?"), L (chapter words, the two outcome facts, the next project), M (every heading-level line: claims, titles, the ticket, quotes, lessons, the subtitle), S (all reading text, notes and drawing labels included). Mono labels are 11px. The shared drawings read the same tokens, with their own sizes as the fallback on v3.

## Copy

Measured the same way on both pages at 1440 × 900: the body prose went from about 1,560 words to about 1,030 (a third less), and no paragraph runs past about 50 words (v3 had several near 80). Every line is Yankun's own sentence, shortened; nothing new is claimed. Lines condensed from v3's drafted copy still need Yankun's rewrite before ship (see `prd-agentic-case-study-v3.md`, Copy).
