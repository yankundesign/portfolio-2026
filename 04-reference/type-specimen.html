From the liner notes

I make invisible systems handleable. In enterprise software, the interface isn't the product — the decision is. My work is in the seam between, where five years of admin-console complexity meets a first-time user at 9 p.m. on a Tuesday.

Voice
Bold, declarative, editorial. The display serif makes a louder statement; the sans keeps reading easy. Best if you want the portfolio to feel like a printed object from the first glance.

A note on execution — the final portfolio will use real hand-drawn marginalia (not the placeholder Caveat font shown here) and hero ink drawings. The paper grain is intentionally subtle; you should only notice it if you lean in.
