## Design Context

### Users

The portfolio primarily serves hiring managers and design leaders who are evaluating Yankun for senior product-design roles. They need to understand the level of craft within seconds and decide whether the work merits an interview within a few minutes. Secondary utility pages, including Ebb's privacy policy, also serve product users and platform reviewers who need direct, trustworthy information without navigating the portfolio experience.

### Brand Personality

Patient, literary, and confidently crafted. The voice is precise and reflective, with enough warmth to feel personal and enough restraint to keep the work authoritative.

### Aesthetic Direction

A printed editorial object rendered in the browser: warm paper, ink blue, Fraunces display and body typography, JetBrains Mono metadata, strict grids, and purposeful asymmetry. The experience is light-mode only. It must avoid generic hero-and-card layouts, scrapbook styling, decorative gradients, ornamental UI chrome, and motion that competes with reading.

### Design Principles

1. Make the content legible before making the composition expressive.
2. Use the two-color ink-and-paper system and the established type hierarchy consistently.
3. Let strict editorial structure create confidence; break the grid only for a specific reason.
4. Keep interaction weighted, quiet, and accessible, with WCAG AA contrast and reduced-motion support.
5. Treat utility pages as part of the same crafted system while making their purpose immediately clear.
