Ebb · Privacy

Privacy policy

Ebb works entirely on your iPhone. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Last updated · August 8, 2026

Data collection

Ebb has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, or cloud synchronization. The app does not send information to the developer or any third party.

On-device data

Ebb stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Selected apps and setup status
  • Intervention history and timestamps
  • Breathing duration, intervention outcomes, and loop state
  • Temporary gate state and your haptics preference

This information is used only to operate Ebb.

Exports

If you choose to export your intervention history, you control where that copy is sent or stored. Ebb does not receive it.

Data deletion

Deleting Ebb removes data stored by Ebb from your device. Previously exported copies remain wherever you chose to save them.
