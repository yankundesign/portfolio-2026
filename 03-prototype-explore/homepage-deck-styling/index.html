C · Restyle in place

The live composition, with a few moves from the deck. Fraunces stays.

Changed from the live desk

  1. The masthead grows from a corner label to a display line, about twice its live size, with the deck’s cube after it.
  2. The cube is the only cobalt on the page.
  3. Chrome is cut to two mono labels at the top and the two-line sign-off at the bottom right. Column numbers and the format, date, gutter and margin readouts go; faint column rulings stay.
  4. Flat warm paper. The cool desk-light vignette goes.

Unchanged

  • Objects, positions, plate frame, register marks and ephemera.
  • One-word object labels and the “open it” cue.
  • The desk-to-notebook transition.

Removed since the first round

  • Hand-drawn baseline under the objects.
  • The thesis line.
  • Italic subtitles under the labels.
  • PP Editorial New.
