# Portfolio Scope — v1

## Target audience

1. **Founding designer** (primary). Leans scrappy and shippy; values range, speed, opinions, and the ability to actually build. Will skim the desk, open the notebook, and decide fast.
2. **Designer at a fast-growth startup** (secondary). Rewards craft + taste + shipped impact. Will read one case study.
3. **AI designer at a mid-size or large company** (tertiary). Will read CHAI in depth.

The site optimizes for (1) without abandoning (2) or (3).

## Positioning — four claims in priority order

1. **I solve complex problems in AI products.** CHAI is the hero evidence. The through-line sentence — *"I make complex, invisible systems handleable"* — is the payoff for this claim.
2. **I have taste and craft.** The site itself is the demo. Desk metaphor, Fraunces, editorial plates, ink-on-paper discipline carry this.
3. **I build with AI, not just design for it.** This portfolio, designed and shipped in one week with Claude Code as a partner, is the artifact. Claim it explicitly in the colophon and on the canvas meta card.
4. **I think interestingly.** Voice, specific choices (cat roulette, dot grid, marginalia), colophon essay.

Experienced / AI-native / good-designer signals are downstream of these four. "Fun" and "not cookie-cutter" are byproducts of specific choices, not goals.

## In scope

- **Desk scene homepage:** Flat-lay desk with notebook (→ works), resume paper (→ CV page), about object (placeholder), cat roulette. Tactile hover; dot grid and marginalia respond to cursor.
- **Canvas / pinboard:** Spatial works view inside the notebook. Hand-composed, viewport-fitted, field-notebook page identity (visible grid, running header/footer, bookplate).
- **Currently + site-build meta card** pinned to the canvas corner. Two signals on one surface: `Currently: [one sentence]` and `This site: designed and shipped in 1 week with Claude Code. Colophon →`.
- **Full case study:** CHAI / Control Hub AI (vertical scroll, 3% → 18% adoption, 86% no-result drop, 14% entry points).
- **Stubbed case studies:** Smart Search, Write-like-Webex, SAP Fieldglass (title + metric + short prose + image).
- **CV page** at `/cv` (Mary Kim–inspired typographic editorial, PDF download).
- **Two journal pages:** "On search", "On endings" — accessible from canvas.
- **Colophon essay** — 300–500 words, specific and unhumble. What Claude did vs. what I did, what broke, what I changed mid-build. Not "with help from AI" — "designed and shipped in 1 week with Claude Code as a partner."
- **404 page** ("This page is blank.")
- **Signature transitions:** desk → canvas portal (notebook opens into world), canvas → project (zoom into cluster). Craft budget concentrates here.
- **Voice sharpened** across case studies: first-person, reflective, cutting. One sharp opinion per paragraph. See `voice.md`.
- **Motion layer** additive across existing surfaces for range proof. Inventory pass before new work; investment weighted toward the two signature transitions.

## Out of scope for v1

- Essays / writing section beyond the two journal pages
- Full B-side of side projects
- Contact form (email link + social handles only)
- Dark mode (paper metaphor breaks it)
- Analytics
- About-me page content (object is placeholder)
- Canvas zoom (pan only for v1)
- Mobile-optimized canvas experience (known hard problem — plan to address in week 2)
- **"Edge surface"** / manifesto page / strong-takes page. Considered and deferred. Range proof comes through motion and visual craft within existing surfaces instead. Revisit in v2 if range reads thin.
