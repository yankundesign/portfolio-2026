# Salesforce Senior UI/UX Designer — General Interview Questions

## Role Thesis

Salesforce needs a senior designer who can own complex platform experiences from discovery through delivery, translate technical requirements into clear workflows, and create scalable patterns across developer-facing tools, internal tooling, and customer surfaces.

## Your Fit in One Sentence

> I have spent six years simplifying complex enterprise systems, and my recent work combines end-to-end platform design, data-rich workflows, AI and agentic experiences, scalable interaction patterns, and close partnership with product and engineering.

## Q&A

### 1. Why are you leaving Cisco?

- **Testing:** Motivation, professionalism, and whether I am moving toward something specific.
- **Answer to say:**
  - "I am not leaving because something is wrong at Cisco. I have had the opportunity to lead meaningful AI work there and grow a lot."
  - "I am ready for a broader level of platform ownership across multiple product areas and user types."
  - "I want to work even closer to product and engineering on foundational experiences that other teams and customers build on."
  - "Salesforce feels like a natural next step because this role combines enterprise complexity, platform thinking, AI, and design at a very large scale."
- **If they push:** "The move is about expanding my scope and impact, not moving away from my current team."

#### Short version

- "Cisco has given me strong experience leading enterprise AI work."
- "My next step is broader platform ownership and more cross-product impact."
- "That is what makes this Salesforce role compelling to me."

### 2. Why Salesforce?

- **Testing:** Company motivation and whether I understand the opportunity.
- **Answer to say:**
  - "Salesforce interests me because it sits at the intersection of enterprise scale, platform complexity, and AI."
  - "My strongest work has been making dense admin systems easier to understand and helping users trust AI inside real workflows."
  - "Salesforce is working on the same larger challenge through Agentforce: how humans and agents work together to create customer value."
  - "I am especially interested in the Platform organization because its design decisions affect developer tools, internal teams, customer-facing products, and the consistency of the wider ecosystem."
  - "That combination of reach, technical depth, and customer impact is difficult to find elsewhere."
- **If they push:** "The phrase from the posting that resonates with me is the connection between technology and trust. My agentic work has taught me that AI only becomes useful when its plans, limits, and actions are understandable."

#### Short version

- "Salesforce combines three things I care about: enterprise scale, platform systems, and trusted AI."
- "The Platform team's decisions affect many products and user types."
- "That is the kind of leverage and complexity I want in my next role."

### 3. Why this role?

- **Testing:** Role fit and whether my experience maps to the actual responsibilities.
- **Answer to say:**
  - "This role matches the work I do best: translating complex technical requirements and data-rich workflows into clear product experiences."
  - "At Cisco, I own end-to-end design for AI experiences in Control Hub, from research and problem framing through prototyping, interaction design, and engineering partnership."
  - "I have also created reusable patterns for contextual AI and agentic workflows rather than treating each feature as a one-off screen."
  - "The opportunity to apply that experience across developer-facing tools, internal tooling, and customer surfaces is exciting to me."
  - "I also like that the role expects a senior designer to influence roadmaps, facilitate reviews, evolve the design system, and stay involved through implementation."
- **If they push:** "The role is a strong match because it needs both systems thinking and hands-on execution. I enjoy defining the model and then staying close enough to make sure the shipped details support it."

#### Short version

- "The role matches my experience with complex enterprise workflows, platform systems, and end-to-end ownership."
- "It also expands my scope into more developer-facing and cross-product experiences."
- "I can contribute immediately while continuing to grow in the direction I want."

### 4. What are you looking for in your next role?

- **Testing:** Priorities, expectations, and likely long-term fit.
- **Answer to say:**
  - "I am looking for a senior IC role where design helps define the product direction, not only execute a roadmap after the major decisions are made."
  - "I want ownership of complex platform problems from discovery and research through detailed design and delivery."
  - "I am looking for close partnership with PM, engineering, research, and other designers, with healthy debate and clear decision-making."
  - "I also want the opportunity to create reusable patterns, contribute to the design system, and raise consistency across more than one product surface."
  - "Most importantly, I want to work on products where better design has a clear effect on customer success and trust."
- **If they push:** "I still want to be hands-on. Broader influence is important to me, but I want that influence to come from understanding the product deeply and making the work concrete."

#### Short version

- "I am looking for broader ownership of complex platform problems."
- "I want design involved early in product decisions and close to engineering through delivery."
- "I also want to create scalable patterns that improve more than one feature or team."

### 5. Where do you see yourself in five years?

- **Testing:** Ambition, self-awareness, and whether Salesforce can support my direction.
- **Answer to say:**
  - "In five years, I want to be a trusted design leader for complex platform and AI products."
  - "I want to be someone teams involve early when the problem is ambiguous, technically difficult, or spans several product areas."
  - "I see myself continuing as a hands-on designer while taking more responsibility for product direction, shared interaction patterns, and the quality bar across teams."
  - "I also want to mentor other designers and help them become more effective partners to product and engineering."
  - "I am less focused on a specific title than on earning broader scope through strong product judgment and measurable customer impact."
- **If they push about management:** "I am open to leadership in different forms, but my current goal is to grow as a senior individual contributor who can lead through the work, influence strategy, and make other designers more effective."

#### Short version

- "I want to become a trusted design leader for complex platform and AI products."
- "I plan to stay hands-on while taking more responsibility for strategy, systems, and mentoring."
- "I care more about earning that scope than reaching a particular title."

## How the Five Answers Connect

- **Leaving Cisco:** I am moving toward broader platform ownership.
- **Salesforce:** It offers enterprise scale, platform leverage, and trusted AI.
- **This role:** It directly matches my experience and expands it into more technical, cross-product surfaces.
- **What I want:** End-to-end ownership, early product influence, close partnership, and scalable systems.
- **Five years:** A hands-on design leader trusted with ambiguous platform and AI problems.

## Language to Mirror From the Posting

- "End-to-end experience"
- "Platform features"
- "Complex technical requirements"
- "Intuitive and scalable"
- "Developer-facing, internal, and customer-facing surfaces"
- "Design system consistency"
- "Accessible implementation"
- "Advocate for the user perspective in roadmap decisions"
- "Humans and agents working together"
- "Technology and trust"

## Avoid Saying

- "I have stopped growing at Cisco."
- "I want to work for a bigger or more prestigious company."
- "I mainly want to focus on AI."
- "I want to move into management quickly."
- "I want more ownership" without explaining ownership of what.
- "Salesforce is the leader in AI" without connecting it to platform design, customers, or trust.

## One Combined Answer if They Ask All Three Motivation Questions Together

- "Cisco has given me the opportunity to lead meaningful enterprise AI work, and I am proud of what I have built there."
- "My next step is broader platform ownership across more products, technical workflows, and user types."
- "Salesforce is compelling because it combines enterprise scale, platform leverage, and the challenge of helping humans and AI agents work together with trust."
- "This specific role matches how I work—from research and problem framing through detailed interaction design, scalable patterns, and engineering delivery."
- "It feels like a role where I can contribute immediately and continue growing toward larger platform-level impact."
