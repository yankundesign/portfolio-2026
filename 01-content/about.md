# About — short version

## Why short

The essay version (archived below) was doing work that other surfaces already do:

- **CV** carries biography, dates, roles.
- **Case studies** carry the work and the impact.
- **Polaroid caption** ("self-portrait, 2026") carries personality at a glance.
- **Colophon** carries the AI-assisted craft story.

That leaves the About with one job: the one paragraph that ties them together. ~30 seconds, no scrolling.

## Three beats only

1. What I do, with one work to anchor it.
2. Where I came from — one tradeoff, not a whole story.
3. What I want next.

> I trained as an architect. **[INVENTED]** First studio, the professor walked in, looked at my model — three weeks of foamcore and balsa — and said, *you've drawn a building, but you haven't decided where the front door is.* I never forgot it.

> I left architecture after school. I kept the systems thinking — scale, threshold, the way a floor plan forces every choice to land somewhere. I gave up the buildings, the eight-year timelines, the fantasy that anything stays put. Software doesn't stay put. That turned out to be the part I liked.
>
> I spent years at SAP Fieldglass on enterprise contracts. Then Cisco Webex, on Control Hub — the admin surface for everything an IT team owns. The settings page had over a thousand controls when I started. Most of them mattered. None of them were findable.

CHAI is the thesis: I built an AI assistant to help admins read the dashboards they already have, not to replace them. Adoption went from 3% to 18%. No-result queries dropped 86%.
